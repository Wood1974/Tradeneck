How TradeDeck works

Escrow, funding & milestones — coming soon

Post a job, compare applicants, and hire. Escrow, funding, and milestones are coming soon.

1. Post the job

A posting takes the trade, the city and county, a scope description and a budget or rate. That is the whole form. Posting is free and there is no limit on how many jobs you can have open.

Escrow, funding, and milestones are coming soon. These features are not available yet.

2. Applicants come to you

Workers and crews apply directly. Nothing is sold, auctioned, or distributed to five contractors at once, because TradeDeck does not make money on leads. You see how many people have applied, and you see each applicant's tier.

3. Compare on track record, not on ad spend

Every profile carries a tier built from on-platform history, not from reviews farmed off-platform or a paid placement. The inputs are objective:

  • Jobs completed on TradeDeck
  • Timeline adherence — did milestones land when promised
  • Cost variance against the original bid
  • Site cleanliness sign-offs at close-out

Transparency runs in every direction. Homeowners see contractor and worker tiers; contractors see worker tiers; workers see who they would be working for before they commit a week to it.
