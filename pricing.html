What's paid

Escrow-funded milestones carry payment processing. That is the only charge on the platform, and it only applies when you choose to run a job with a draw schedule.

The practical effect: a contractor who bids ten jobs and wins two pays TradeDeck on the two. On a per-lead platform they would have paid for all ten, plus whatever the other bidders paid for the same homeowner.

Common questions

Is there a subscription?

No. There is no membership tier, no monthly fee and no annual plan.

Do you charge per lead?

No. TradeDeck does not sell leads, which is the core difference from directory platforms that charge contractors on delivery whether or not the job closes.

What do you actually charge for?

Payment processing on escrow-funded milestones. If you never use a draw schedule, you never pay TradeDeck anything.

Are there fees to contact someone?

No. Contact between parties on a job is free and not masked or metered.
