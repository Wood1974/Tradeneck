The deposit problem

The standard ask is a deposit before anything starts. Once that money is gone, all the leverage sits on one side of the table. If the crew stops showing up, your only options are patience or a lawyer.

The "who did I just hire" problem

Directory sites rank whoever pays the most for the placement. That is not a quality signal, it is an ad auction. TradeDeck ranks on what actually happened on previous jobs: how many were completed, whether milestones hit their dates, how close the final cost landed to the original bid, and whether the site got left clean.

You also see the tier of the individual workers, not just the company name on the truck.
